> The sets of natural numbers, integers, rational numbers, real numbers, and complex numbers are denoted by N, Z, Q, R, and C, respectively. We write Rn for the n-dimensional real vector space consisting of all length-n vectors x = (x1, x2, . . . , xn ) with xi ∈ R. The length of such a vector x is ‖x ‖ = √ x2 1 + x2 2 + · · · + x2 n , which is also denoted ‖x ‖2 and called the Euclidean length, Euclidean norm, or `2-norm. The infinity norm of x is ‖x ‖∞ = maxi |xi |. The set Zn denotes the subset of Rn consisting of all length-n vectors whose components are integers.
